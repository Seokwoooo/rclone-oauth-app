Privacy policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This app does not collect or store personal information. Google Drive data is used only for transfers between rclone on the user's computer and the user's own Google Drive. The app does not send the data to third-party servers, store it there, or share it with third parties.

The app's use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access in your Google Account permissions. For questions, use this repository's GitHub Issues.
